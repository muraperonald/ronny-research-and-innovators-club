About R-RIC

Students who believe the best way to learn is to ask good questions, then build the answer.

Meet the committee
Our background

From a study group to a research community

R-RIC began in 2023 as a handful of first-year students meeting in the library to help each other through programming assignments. We kept asking "what if we built that for real?", and the study group became a project team.

Today the Ronny Research & Innovation Club has more than 180 members from computing, engineering, business and the sciences. We run research workshops, mentor project teams, compete in hackathons and host an annual research & innovation symposium where students present their work to lecturers and industry guests.

The R in our name stands for Ronny, after the club's founder, and for the research mindset we bring to everything we build.

Vision, mission and values

Our Vision

A campus where every student can turn curiosity into research, and research into innovation that improves lives.

Our Mission

To build a supportive community that teaches practical research and technical skills, connects students with mentors, and gives them real projects to lead.

Our Values

Curiosity, integrity in research, open collaboration, inclusion of every background and skill level, and learning by doing.

What we aim to do

Club objectives

  1. Build research skills Teach literature reviews, experiment design, data analysis and academic writing.
  2. Grow technical talent Run hands-on workshops in programming, AI, IoT, cybersecurity and design.
  3. Turn ideas into prototypes Support mentored project teams from first idea to working demo.
  4. Connect with industry Bring in speakers, organise industry visits and open doors to internships.
  5. Showcase student work Host the annual symposium and help members publish and present.

What members get

  • A project team
    Work alongside students from other programmes.
  • A mentor
    Get paired with a senior student or lecturer.
  • Lab access
    Use club hardware kits, sensors and dev boards.
  • Certificates
    Earn certificates for workshops and projects.

Award · Innovation Expo 2026

Seed Category Winner: USD 5,000 for student innovation

R-RIC innovators were recognised at the Innovation Expo & Conference 2026, an event backed by the Ministry of ICT, Postal and Courier Services and POTRAZ under the ITU Acceleration Centres Network.

Competing in the Smart Communities, Green Technologies and Future Skills track, our team was named Seed Category Winner and received a USD 5,000 award, presented on stage by the Minister of ICT, Postal and Courier Services. The seed funding will help the team move their project from prototype towards a working product.

Recognition · Innovation Expo 2026

Certificates of recognition for our innovators

At the closing ceremony, outstanding innovators, including R-RIC members, were called on stage to receive certificates of recognition alongside the Minister of ICT, Postal and Courier Services and expo officials.

The recognition reflects months of work in our project teams: researching real community problems, building prototypes and pitching them to judges. Want to be on that stage next year?

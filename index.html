Who we are

A home for curious minds on campus

R-RIC is a student-run club open to every programme and year of study. Whether you have never written code or you are already shipping apps, you will find people to learn from and projects to join.

Each semester we run:

  • Hands-on research and coding workshops
  • Paper reading circles and research talks
  • Hackathons and an annual research & innovation symposium
  • Mentored project teams that build real prototypes
